texture, empty animation and inventory item models at runtime. Its native worn

of Ars Nouveau 5.13.3's textures/item/wither.png red; all other pixels remain
unchanged. This asset retains its upstream rights outside the MIT source license.

The Netherite Enchanter's Sword references Ars Nouveau's native sword geometry,
item display transforms, animation resource and texture at runtime. Its hilt UVs
are recolored to Netherite tones in memory, and the crystal blade is tinted
purple during rendering. No native sword model, texture or animation file is
bundled. No Violetgold Equipment artwork or geometry is bundled or referenced.
